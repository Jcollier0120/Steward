The text reader's model (ocr-model-v1)

These files are PaddleOCR's PP-OCRv6 tiny models, by the PaddlePaddle Authors
(https://github.com/PaddlePaddle/PaddleOCR), licensed under the Apache License,
Version 2.0 (LICENSE.txt), with changes by Castellan under the same license:

- det.onnx: PP-OCRv6 tiny's text detector, as PaddlePaddle publishes it at
  https://huggingface.co/PaddlePaddle/PP-OCRv6_tiny_det_onnx (revision
  2ba1506c0380b8f0b03dd142459aac66d4421f6c), its input and output dimensions named.

- rec.onnx: PP-OCRv6 tiny's text recognizer, from
  https://huggingface.co/PaddlePaddle/PP-OCRv6_tiny_rec_onnx (revision
  2612ab37152ae0a677521bae4e1e3d4fb4cf7c30). Changed: its head replaced by one
  over the characters in charset.txt; its last average pooling made over the
  height alone, written as a mean, and the convolution after it dilated, so it
  takes a step every 4 columns rather than 8; the whole fine-tuned on text
  drawn as screens and documents draw it; the space's output raised by 1; and
  its softmax removed.

- charset.txt: the recognizer's characters, class 1 onwards.

No training data is included. The training lines were generated, and drawn
with the fonts installed on Windows; none of them is shipped here.
